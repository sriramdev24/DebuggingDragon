How the SkillSwap DNA Matching Works

1. Direct 2-Person Reciprocal Matching

When Student A knows Python and wants UI Design, and Student B knows UI Design and wants Python, the matching engine calculates a weighted compatibility score based on reciprocity (50%), proficiency tier satisfaction (25%), and shared available time slots (25%).

2. Circular 3-Way Exchange Pods

When two students cannot exchange directly, SkillSwap DNA discovers directed cycle loops: Student A → Student B → Student C → Student A. Everyone teaches 1 skill and learns 1 skill without money.
